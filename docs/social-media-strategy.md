# Social Media Strategy & Editorial Operations

## 1. Dual-Channel Architecture

The institution operates a balanced dual-channel model:

| Channel | Handle / Platform | Purpose | Primary Audience | Editorial Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Official Institutional** | `@ceknpy.official` / Instagram & LinkedIn | Formal circulars, accreditation milestones, placement reports, administrative directives, faculty honors. | Prospective parents, university authorities, recruiting companies, alumni. | Authoritative, dignified, precise, aspirational. |
| **Student Community Storytelling** | `@humansofcek` / Instagram | Human-interest student journeys, project build vlogs, campus friendships, hostel culture, cultural events, sports. | Current students, young prospective applicants, recent alumni. | Warm, relatable, authentic, creative, documentary-style. |

## 2. Content Pillars
1. **Engineering in Action**: Final-year capstone prototypes, IEEE SB workshops, IEDC hardware incubations, robotics demos.
2. **Campus Culture**: Arts festival, Onam celebrations, tech-fest symposiums, sports meets.
3. **Humans of CEK Spotlight**: In-depth personal profiles of students, grassroots inventors, and inspiring mentors.
4. **Admissions & Guidance**: KEAM preparation tips, campus tour videos, NRI quota FAQs.
5. **Alumni Horizons**: Global graduate outcomes, higher education at premier institutes, startup founders.

## 3. Workflow: From Discovery to Publication
1. **Intake**: Submission via Student Union / IEEE / IEDC / `@humansofcek` scouts.
2. **Attribution & Rights Check**: Confirm consent from all featured subjects; record photography ownership.
3. **Editorial Gatekeeper**: Review summary, factual claims, and compliance in IMS Editorial module.
4. **Institutional Clearance**: Formal sign-off by Public Relations Officer / Content Editor.
5. **Publish & Measure**: Simultaneous release with cross-linking between web portal and social channels.
