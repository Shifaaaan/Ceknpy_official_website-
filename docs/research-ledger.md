## 2. Research Decisions & Design Adaptations

1. **Brand Identity & Color Palette**:
   - Deep Institutional Sapphire Navy (`#0f2942`) paired with Engineering Teal/Cyan (`#0284c7`) and Warm Kerala Amber/Gold (`#d97706`).
   - Reflects technical rigor, maritime coastal heritage of Kollam/Karunagappally, and government prestige.

2. **Humans of CEK Curation**:
   - Positioned as a dedicated "Campus Voices" editorial stream, clearly differentiated from official administrative notices.
   - Enforces attribution, verification tags, and explicit consent tracking before stories are elevated to the homepage.

3. **Academic Integrity & KTU Regulations**:
   - Real-world 75% attendance threshold with automated warnings for condonation ranges (60–75%) and critical shortage (<60%).
   - Timetable conflicts guard against simultaneous teacher, room, or batch double-booking.
