# Content Strategy & Editorial Framework

## 1. Editorial Principles
- **Authenticity over Hype**: Ground claims in verified institutional programs, genuine student achievements, and corroborated activities.
- **Traceable Attribution**: Every story, news item, and circular must declare its primary origin (e.g., KTU notification, IEDC Cell, IEEE SB, or `@humansofcek`).
- **Privacy & Media Rights**: No photo or student interview is published without recorded consent and copyright clearance.

## 2. Homepage Content Hierarchy
1. **Header & Institutional Banner**: CEK crest, IHRD governance badge, KTU affiliation, quick navigation to departments, admissions, campus life, notices, and the IMS portal.
2. **Hero Section**: High-impact institutional statement highlighting 25+ years of engineering excellence in Karunagappally, NBA accreditation, and top placement track record.
3. **Institutional Pillars (Quick Metrics)**: 6 Engineering & Science departments, 1500+ students, active IEEE/IEDC cells, NBA accredited.
4. **Academic Departments Carousel/Grid**: Direct deep-dive into CSE, ECE, EEE, ME, AI&DS, and Basic Sciences.
5. **Authentic Campus Storytelling ("Humans of CEK & Campus Life")**: Curated spotlights showcasing student innovation, national hackathon wins, cultural celebrations (Onam, Arts Fest), and technical leadership.
6. **Verified Circulars & Academic Notices**: Filterable by Academic, Examination, and General with dates and official reference numbers.
7. **Admissions Callout**: KEAM merit ranks, NRI quota guidelines, and enquiry form.
8. **Institutional Footer**: Thodiyoor campus address, contact coordinates, emergency lines, accreditation disclosures, and governance links.
